## The organizing principle: follow builders, not influencers

Borrowed from Zara Zhang (张咋啦), who put it best: **关注产品人，不关注媒体人** — follow
product people, not media people. Her related claims, which are worth taking seriously:

- Only trust recommendations from people who *deeply use* the product they're recommending
- 只要掌握信息源，中国和硅谷没有信息差 — with the right sources, there's no information gap
  between China and Silicon Valley
- The way into AI work is to start by *using* AI tools

This matters for your source list specifically. The accounts in the "Lab leadership" section
below have the highest follower counts and the **worst** response opportunities — ten thousand
people reply to every @sama post within minutes, and your take is invisible. Builders posting
real numbers to 3k followers are where a reply gets seen and where your angle is additive.

Weight accordingly. The ⚡ column marks accounts where you regularly think *"right, but they're
missing something"* — those generate the best ideas, and they're rarely the famous ones.

**Put your ⚡ marks in `sources/x-accounts.local.md`, not here.** This file is public; the
local one is gitignored. Your private read on who's wrong about what shouldn't ship to GitHub
under your name. `/scan` and `/oracle` read both.
