# The Vault

Idea backlog. `/oracle` appends every idea you don't pick here, with its source and date.
Append-only — nothing gets deleted, because a thin idea in March is sometimes the right idea
in July.

选题库。`/oracle` 会把你没选走的选题连同来源和日期追加到这里。只增不删——三月看着单薄的
选题,七月可能正好用上。

Format:

```
## <the angle, stated as a claim>
- **Banked:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Source:** <where it came from — quote the spike>
- **Specifics on hand:** <numbers/names/stories already available>
- **Tension:** <what someone would push back on>
- **Status:** open | used YYYY-MM-DD | retired
```

# 📮 External sources · 外部源专区

> Ideas mined from **your email / newsletters / other third-party sources** go here, with
> the data source named in `Source`. Kept separate from the `/oracle` track ideas.
> Filled by the `content-scout` skill — it reads your own inbox locally; nothing is uploaded.
>
> 凡是从**邮箱 / newsletter / 其他第三方数据源**挖出的选题一律入这个区,`Source` 写明数据源,
> 与赛道选题分开管理。由 `content-scout` skill 填充(读你自己的邮箱,本地进行,不上传)。
> Append-only.

# 🎙️ Meetings · 会议记录专区

> Ideas mined from **meeting notes** (Granola exports, voice memos, interview transcripts).
> The judgments you blurted out, the claims someone pushed back on, the analogy you keep
> reusing — those are content spikes. Filled by `content-scout` from your local exports;
> nothing is uploaded.
>
> 从**会议记录(Granola、语音备忘、访谈转写)**里挖出的选题入这个区。会议里你脱口而出的判断、
> 被人追问的观点、反复讲的类比——都是 content spike。由 `content-scout` 读你本地导出的
> 会议纪要填充,不上传。Append-only.
